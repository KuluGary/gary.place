Hey! I hope everyone’s had a good spooky season. It’s my favorite time of year: orange leaves, crisp air, tasty treats… After a very hot summer, I think everyone appreciated a breather.

On my end, there’s a few things to talk about that happened these past few months, so let’s get to it shall we?
## Game development

This October I participated in my first game jam ever. [molegato](https://molegato.com/) opened a [Halloween game jam](https://molegato.com/halloween-gamejam-2026/) and myself and a handful of other developers made some little games. Everyone did a really great job so please try them all.

My entry was called [Theodore’s Great Hunt](https://kulugary.itch.io/theodores-great-hunt). It’s a 10 minute riff on Duck Hunt, with a little bit of a twist added to it. If you want to, please play it and share your thoughts! I also wrote a [full-length post](https://gary.place/blog/my-first-game-jam) about it on my blog that you can read if you’d like to.
## Learning Japanese

So, uh… I’m learning Japanese?? Mid-way through October I started to use online resources to bein studying. I looked around the best ways to do it, and the one study guide I found that I’m following is [Tofugu](https://www.tofugu.com/), a website full of resources on the Japanese language.
