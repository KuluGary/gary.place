Privacy Policy

This website is a static website generated with Eleventy and hosted by Netlify.

Data Controller

This website is independently operated by an individual based in Spain.

For questions concerning personal information or requests relating to your data protection rights, you can contact me at:

webmastr@gary.place.

I do not use user accounts, advertising, or third-party website analytics. I do not collect personal information when you simply browse this website. However, certain technical information may be processed by the hosting provider, and information is processed by Buttondown if you subscribe to the newsletter.

Hosting

This website is hosted by Netlify. When you visit the website, Netlify may process technical information associated with your request, such as your IP address, browser, device, and the pages or resources you request. This processing is part of providing and securing the hosting service.

For more information, please see Netlify's Privacy Statement.

Newsletter

This website offers an optional email newsletter. The newsletter is managed and delivered using Buttondown.

The subscription form is embedded directly on this website. When you subscribe, your email address is sent to Buttondown and stored on its servers so that I can manage your subscription and send you newsletters. Buttondown may also collect technical information associated with your subscription, such as your IP address and referral information.

The legal basis for processing your email address is your consent, in accordance with Article 6(1)(a) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

I have disabled email open and link-click tracking. I do not use this information to monitor how subscribers interact with newsletters.

You can withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every newsletter.

Your subscription information is retained for as long as you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, Buttondown handles the deletion or retention of your information according to its own data retention practices and applicable legal obligations.

Buttondown is based in the United States. Personal information may therefore be transferred to and processed in the United States. Such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable.

For more information, please see:

  • Buttondown's Privacy Policy
  • Buttondown's GDPR information
  • Buttondown's Data Processing Agreement

Your Data Protection Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Request data portability, where applicable.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact me at webmaster@gary.place.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) if you believe your personal information has been processed unlawfully.

You can find more information at www.aepd.es.

I do not control the privacy practices of these third-party services.

This website uses locally hosted font files. Your browser downloads these files directly from this website. The website does not request fonts directly from Google Fonts.

External links

Articles may contain links to external websites. When you follow one of these links, the external website's own privacy policy applies.

Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy if the website or the services it uses change. The current version will always be published on this page.
